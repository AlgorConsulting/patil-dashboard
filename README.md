A representational sample built by ALGOR Consulting Group (https://www.algor-consulting.com/). All production, quality, dispatch and inventory figures are synthetic and illustrative; no client data is used.

Tabs: Executive Summary (cross-plant scorecard), Production & Moulds, Quality & Inspection, Dispatch & Orders, Materials & Yard. Filters: financial year (Apr-Mar), period, zone, plant, product line, customer segment.
